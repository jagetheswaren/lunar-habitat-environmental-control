Architectural Standards & Invariants

1. Double-Entry Accounting Invariant: All financial entries are processed via the AccountingEngineService ensuring Total Debit == Total Credit before committing to the general ledger.

2. Autonomous Life Support Control: Telemetry ingestion pipelines automatically inspect threshold violations and adjust CO2 scrubbers without latency.

3. Role-Based Access Control: Strict separation of roles across ADMIN, HABITAT_OPERATOR, ACCOUNTANT, TENANT_USER, and VIEWER.

4. Precision Assurance: All monetary allocations and resource transactions utilize BigDecimal (19,4) precision.
